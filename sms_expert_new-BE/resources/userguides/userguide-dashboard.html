Send SMS

Send individual or bulk SMS messages to your recipients.

Send SMS Page

How to Send an SMS

  • Navigate to Send SMS from the sidebar menu
  • Select your Sender ID from the dropdown
  • Enter the recipient phone number(s) in international format
  • Type your message in the text area
  • Review the estimated cost
  • Click Send SMS to send immediately

Message Character Limits

Message Type Characters per SMS Notes
Standard (GSM) 160 characters Basic Latin characters, numbers
Unicode 70 characters Emojis, Arabic, Chinese, etc.
Concatenated 153/67 per part Long messages split into parts

Phone Number Formats

Enter recipient phone numbers in international format for best delivery rates:

Format Example Status
International with + +447700900123 Recommended
International without + 447700900123 Accepted
Local format 07700900123 May require country code

Bulk SMS Entry

Send to multiple recipients by entering numbers in one of these formats:

  • One per line - Enter each phone number on a separate line
  • Comma separated - Enter numbers separated by commas (e.g., +447700900123,+447700900124)
  • Select from Groups - Choose a pre-saved contact group

Sender ID Options

Virtual Number

Use your assigned virtual number as sender ID - recipients can reply

Alphanumeric ID

Use your brand name (up to 11 characters) - no replies possible

Shortcode

Use an assigned shortcode for promotional messages

Important

Ensure sufficient wallet balance before sending. Each SMS part is charged separately. The cost is calculated before sending and displayed on screen.

Pro Tip

Watch the character counter as you type - it shows remaining characters and number of SMS parts your message will use.

Schedule SMS

Schedule messages to be sent at a specific date and time.

Schedule Message section on the Send SMS page

How to Schedule an SMS

  • Go to Send SMS and fill in your message (recipients, sender and message text)
  • In the Schedule Message section, set the date next to the "Send at" button
  • Choose the hour and minute for delivery
  • Click the Send at button to schedule the message

Scheduling Options

Option Description
Date Selection Choose any future date from the calendar picker
Time Selection Set the exact hour and minute for delivery (24-hour format)

Managing Scheduled Messages

View Scheduled

See all pending scheduled messages in Sent SMS section

Note

Scheduled messages are deducted from your wallet at the time of sending, not when scheduled. Your balance is checked again before the message is sent.

Error Cause Solution
Invalid Number Phone number format is incorrect Verify number and use international format
Number Not Reachable Phone is switched off or out of coverage Retry later or contact recipient

Auto-Actions for Received SMS

Configure automatic actions through Keywords management:

  • Auto-Reply - Send an automatic response when a keyword is received
  • Email Forward - Forward incoming SMS to specified email addresses
  • SMS Forward - Forward incoming SMS to another phone number
  • Webhook - Send data to your application via HTTP POST
Note

Received messages are stored for 90 days. Export important messages for long-term storage.

Keywords Management

Manage your keywords, configure auto-responders, and set up forwarding rules.

Keywords List

Keyword Features

SMS Responder

Auto-reply to incoming SMS messages

Email Forwarder

Forward SMS to email addresses

SMS Forwarder

Forward SMS to another phone

Business Card

Send digital business cards

Creating a Keyword

  • Navigate to Keywords from the sidebar
  • Click Create New Keyword
  • Enter your keyword text (e.g., INFO, HELP, JOIN)
  • Select the virtual number to associate with
  • Configure the action type (responder, forwarder, etc.)
  • Save the keyword configuration

Keyword Action Types

Action Description Use Case
SMS Responder Automatically send a reply SMS when keyword is received Information requests, confirmations
Email Forwarder Forward received SMS to one or more email addresses Lead capture, support tickets
SMS Forwarder Forward received SMS to another phone number Team notifications, alerts
Webhook Send message data to your application URL CRM integration, automation
Business Card Send vCard with contact details Networking, contact sharing

Keyword Best Practices

Tips for Effective Keywords

Use short, memorable keywords (3-8 characters). Avoid common words that might be accidentally triggered. Test your keyword setup before promoting it to customers.

Example Keyword Setups

Keyword Action Response
INFO SMS Responder "Thank you for your interest! Visit our website at www.example.com for more details."
STOP Opt-Out Handler "You have been unsubscribed. Text START to re-subscribe."
SUPPORT Email Forwarder Forwards to support@company.com

Buy SMS Credits

Purchase SMS credits to top up your wallet.

Buy SMS Page

How to Purchase Credits

  • Navigate to Buy SMS from the sidebar
  • Enter the amount you wish to purchase (minimum £100, + VAT)
  • Click "Buy SMS" to generate your invoice
  • Receive your invoice by email with payment instructions
  • Complete payment by bank transfer (or card/PayPal up to your account limit)
  • Once cleared funds are received, credits are added to your wallet (please allow up to 24 hours)

Payment Methods

Method Processing Time Notes
PayPal Instant Secure payment via PayPal account or guest checkout
Credit/Debit Card Instant Visa, Mastercard, American Express accepted
BACS Transfer 1-3 Business Days UK bank transfer - reference number provided
Invoice On Receipt Available for approved business accounts

Tip

Contact your account manager for custom pricing on high-volume accounts. Volume pricing can significantly reduce your per-SMS costs.
